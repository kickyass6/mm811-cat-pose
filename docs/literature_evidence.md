| 5 | From Synthetic to Real: Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for Animal Pose Estimation · Chen Li and Gim Hee Lee (2021) [Original paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Li_From_Synthetic_to_Real_Unsupervised_Domain_Adaptation_for_Animal_Pose_CVPR_2021_paper.html) | Synthetic source animals and real target animals. | 6 | Few-Shot Keypoint Detection With Uncertainty Learning for Unseen Species · Changsheng Lu and Piotr Koniusz (2022) [Original paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Lu_Few-Shot_Keypoint_Detection_With_Uncertainty_Learning_for_Unseen_Species_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf) | Animal Pose, over 6,000 instances from five species, plus CUB and NABird. | 7 | ScarceNet: Animal Pose Estimation With Scarce Annotations · Chen Li and Gim Hee Lee (2023) [Original paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Li_ScarceNet_Animal_Pose_Estimation_With_Scarce_Annotations_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf) | AP-10K with 5–25 labeled images per species plus unlabeled images. At 25, it reached 0.681 versus 0.588. | The method uses unlabeled target-domain images and pools species. | Low-label data budgets and context for controlled supervised baselines. |
| 8 | Pretraining boosts out-of-domain robustness for pose estimation · Alexander Mathis et al. (2021) [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.11229) | Horse-10 with 30 horses. Held-out horse identities and Horse-C corruptions. | MobileNetV2, ResNet and EfficientNet pose models initialized from ImageNet. Normalized pose error and PCK. | Stronger pretrained classifiers tended to generalize better to unseen horses and corruptions. Pretraining also helped in cross-species checks. | Video-based horse splits and classifier architecture comparisons differ from a fixed cat backbone comparison. | Distinguish within-species test generalization from transfer to other species. |
| 9 | CLAMP: Prompt-Based Contrastive Learning for Connecting Language and Animal Pose · Xu Zhang et al. (2023) [Original paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhang_CLAMP_Prompt-Based_Contrastive_Learning_for_Connecting_Language_and_Animal_Pose_CVPR_2023_paper.html) | AP-10K and Animal-Pose. The AP-10K few-shot setting uses 20 training images per species. | CLAMP aligns body-part text prompts with image features using spatial and feature contrastive losses. OKS AP on AP-10K. | ResNet-50 CLAMP reached 72.9 AP on supervised AP-10K versus 70.9 for the CLIP-initialized SimpleBaseline. | The method uses an additional vision-language source. Its gains do not identify the value of COCO human keypoints. | Future route for distinguishing paws with similar appearance. |
| 10 | Probabilistic Prompt Distribution Learning for Animal Pose Estimation · Jiyong Rao et al. (2025) [Original paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Rao_Probabilistic_Prompt_Distribution_Learning_for_Animal_Pose_Estimation_CVPR_2025_paper.html) | AP-10K (10,015 labeled images) and Animal Kingdom. Includes supervised and held-out-species evaluation. | Probabilistic learned prompts built from pretrained vision-language features. AP on AP-10K and PCK on Animal Kingdom. | Diverse prompt samples and spatial fusion improved reported supervised and zero-shot pose results. | Prompt supervision and multi-species experiments differ from a cat-only ResNet study. | Context for visually ambiguous body parts and long tails. |

| 15 | Multi-animal pose estimation, identification and tracking with DeepLabCut · Jessy Lauer et al.
